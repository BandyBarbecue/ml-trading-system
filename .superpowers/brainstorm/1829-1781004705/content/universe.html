How large should the trading universe be?

More stocks = more data, more diversification, more ML signal — but more complexity and storage.

A

Curated ~20–50 stocks

Hand-picked high-liquidity names: mega-caps, sector ETFs. Manageable. Each stock gets deep news coverage. Good for learning. Limited cross-sectional diversity.

B

S&P 500 (~500 stocks)

The professional standard. Enough stocks to rank cross-sectionally, diversify, and find real signal. News coverage is broad. Data is 5–10× heavier. This is where quant strategies come alive.

C

Multi-asset (stocks + crypto + ETFs)

Diversify across asset classes. Crypto adds 24/7 data and high volatility. Harder to build a unified model — different regimes, different liquidity. More complex, but interesting.

My recommendation: S&P 500. It's the sweet spot — large enough for cross-sectional ML, liquid enough that news actually moves prices, and well-studied enough that you can validate your results against academic benchmarks.
