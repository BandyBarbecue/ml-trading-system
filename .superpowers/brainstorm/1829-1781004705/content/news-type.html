What kind of news signal?

Each type requires different data sources and NLP approaches. You can combine later — pick your primary first.

A

News headline sentiment

NLP on financial headlines: "Apple beats earnings", "Fed raises rates", "CEO resigns". Classify each headline as bullish/bearish/neutral and score stocks daily. Source: Alpaca News API (you already have the key).

B

Earnings surprises

Did the company beat or miss analyst consensus EPS? Beat = bullish catalyst. Miss = bearish. Quantified, not fuzzy. Strong academic backing — post-earnings drift is one of the most persistent anomalies. Source: Financial Modeling Prep (free tier).

C

Both — fused into one signal

News sentiment + earnings surprise combined into a single "fundamental momentum" score. More robust than either alone. Slightly more complex pipeline. Recommended — the two signals are complementary and both free.

My recommendation: C. Headline sentiment catches intraday reactions; earnings surprise captures multi-week drift. Together they cover different time horizons of the same fundamental story.
