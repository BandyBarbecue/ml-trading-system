What does "most powerful" mean to you?

This shapes the entire architecture. Pick the one that resonates most.

A

Maximum risk-adjusted returns

Best Sharpe ratio. Capital preservation matters as much as gains. The goal is consistent edge, not home runs. This is how professional quant funds think.

B

Most sophisticated architecture

Use ML, alternative data, regime detection. The goal is to learn cutting-edge techniques and build something genuinely impressive — returns follow sophistication.

C

Maximum robustness

Works across market regimes — bull, bear, sideways, volatile. Doesn't blow up. Survives 2008, 2020, 2022. Stress-tested, regime-aware.

D

Fully working in 2 months

Ship something real and complete. A deployable paper-trading system with live signals, monitoring, and a track record — even if the strategy is simpler.

Note: these aren't mutually exclusive long-term — but picking a primary goal keeps the architecture honest.
