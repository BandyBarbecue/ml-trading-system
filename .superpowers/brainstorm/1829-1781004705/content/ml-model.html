Section 4: ML Model

LightGBM learns which combination of your six signals predicts the top-performing stocks. Walk-forward keeps it honest. Dynamic IC weighting keeps it adaptive.

Why LightGBM?

Gradient boosting = an ensemble of decision trees where each new tree corrects the errors of the previous ones. Analogy: 100 junior analysts each specialising in different patterns, combining votes.

  • Outperforms neural nets on tabular financial data (well-established)
  • Handles missing values natively — not every stock has earnings data every day
  • Feature importance built-in — you can see which signal contributed most
  • Trains in seconds on 500 stocks, easy to retrain monthly

Target variable

The model predicts the 20-day forward cross-sectional return rank — a number from 0 to 1 representing which percentile of the S&P 500 a stock will land in over the next month.

Why ranks, not raw returns? Ranks are more stationary — the model learns "this pattern predicts being in the top 20%" rather than "this predicts +3.7%". Much more robust across different market conditions.

With 5 years of data and a 2-year training window: ~3 years of out-of-sample backtest predictions. That's enough to validate through the 2022 bear market, the 2023 recovery, and the 2024–25 AI bull run.

Dynamic IC Weighting — how the model stays adaptive

IC = Information Coefficient = Spearman correlation between a feature's daily values and actual 20-day forward returns. A feature with IC = 0.05 is genuinely predictive. IC = 0 means noise. IC goes negative when a factor stops working.

NORMAL MARKET (2023–2024)
Momentum 12mIC 0.07 ↑↑
News sentimentIC 0.05 ↑
Earnings surpriseIC 0.06 ↑
Mean reversionIC 0.01 →
VOLATILE MARKET (2022 bear)
Momentum 12mIC -0.03 ↓↓
News sentimentIC 0.08 ↑↑
Earnings surpriseIC 0.05 ↑
Mean reversionIC 0.06 ↑↑

Each month, before retraining, we compute 60-day rolling IC for all features. Features with higher recent IC get upweighted as LightGBM feature_weight inputs. The model automatically leans on what's been working lately — without any manual intervention.

OUTPUT OF THIS LAYER

One predicted_score (0–1) for each of 500 stocks, refreshed daily. Top 25 by score are passed to the portfolio construction layer.
